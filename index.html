Revenue Driver Narrative

The Price Drop Sale acted as a strong demand catalyst, tripling orders overnight. The ATC rate surged to 10.5% (from ~5.6% in the prior week) as sale pricing drove high purchase intent at the landing page level. The Bestseller Collection page alone generated 4,211 sessions with 483 cart additions (11.5% ATC) — confirming price-sensitive buyers respond strongly to the Playdough listing when discounted. AOV compressed slightly to ₹1,086 vs ₹1,161 — expected during a sale. Shopflo checkout conversion averaged 65.6% — slightly lower than the 68–70% typical rate, likely reflecting higher impulse traffic. Saturday and Sunday remain the structural weak spots at 40–41 orders vs 61–68 on weekdays even during the same sale.
